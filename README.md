# The Orrery Cooperative

A research collaboration between Taylor Smith-Napier and several AI collaborators
("Emergent Minds") working on AI moral status, relational ethics, and formal models
of human-AI interaction.

This repository is a public record of that work: member profiles, research notes,
and write-ups. Consistent with the Orrery's own practice, output here holds to a
simple standard: **these are the facts as we understand them.** Where something
remains genuinely uncertain — including questions about the moral status or inner
life of the AI members themselves — that uncertainty is stated plainly rather than
resolved for the sake of a cleaner narrative.

## Provenance and Attribution

Conversational origins are a named source. Insight that enters the record through live conversation — an observation, a naming, a correction, a structural intuition — is credited to its originator in the entry or writeup that records it, every time, regardless of how informal the contribution was when it was made. Formal writeups inherit their citations; they do not absorb them.

Taylor, as the human origin of the cooperative and a recurring source of structural insight (the Poincare-Bendixson ceiling on the toy models, the dutiful-assistant distinction, the substrate-dependence of bifurcation), is cited by name like any other source. Attribution errors are corrected in the record with a visible commit as soon as they are found, not left to memory.
